I’m a product/UX research leader and HCI researcher working across industry and academic research.

In industry, I build 0→1 research capabilities and lead foundational mixed-methods programs that shape research agendas, product strategy, and roadmaps. In my academic work, I study collective sensemaking, trust, and credibility in fast-moving, high-stakes information environments.

Current research

My primary empirical setting is severe-weather livestreaming and the emerging ecosystem of weatherfluencers. I study how creators make evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, and revision visible as events unfold, and how audiences use livestream chat to question, corroborate, compare, and continuously evaluate those interpretations.

I’m also studying what happens across repeated events: why audiences return to particular weatherfluencers, how trust accumulates or erodes, and how local observations, ground-truth reports, moderation, vouching, and correction shape collective interpretation over time.
